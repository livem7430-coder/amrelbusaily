## Priorities / الأولويات
1. Commercial geo + services (buying intent) — geo pages + service guides first.
2. High-volume informational (tools, how-tos) — builds authority + internal-link weight up to money pages.
3. AI/answer-engine topics — future-proofing, matches AEO positioning.

## Internal-link rules / قواعد الربط الداخلي
- Every geo article links up to its market landing page (/ar/seo-expert-<market>) and the homepage /ar.
- Every service/tool/how-to article links to the closest money page (/ar/seo-expert, /ar/seo-services or a geo page).
- Anchor text is descriptive ("خبير سيو في السعودية"), never "اضغط هنا".
