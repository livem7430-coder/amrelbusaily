# SEO content checklist
Reviewed: 2026-10-01. Monthly policy review, plus relevant official updates between reviews. Applies to new content and revisions; articles and SEO only, no design changes.

## What Google penalizes
Do not create scaled pages primarily to manipulate rankings, swap country names into duplicate pages, stuff keywords, invent reviews/results or hide content from readers. Automation does not excuse poor quality. Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies

## People-first content
Choose a real store-owner problem and one primary intent. Check the complete existing topic map before adding a page. Provide a decision, practical steps, a clearly labelled hypothetical example when useful, limits and official sources. Do not invent search volume, client outcomes or ranking guarantees. There is no safe daily quota or mandatory article length. Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content

## E-E-A-T on the page
- Experience: use only documented first-hand work. Label hypothetical examples; do not invent clients or tests.
- Expertise: explain what to do, why, constraints and how to validate; cite current official sources.
- Authoritativeness: accurate Amr Elbusaily byline and an existing author/background link, not fabricated endorsements.
- Trust: state material uncertainty, keep visible information and structured data consistent, date substantive reviews and avoid sales/ranking promises. E-E-A-T itself is not one specific ranking factor.
Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content

## Before publication
- [ ] Distinct primary keyword/intent, compared with existing published topics; no stuffing or near-duplicate page.
- [ ] Correct Arabic-first audience fit; English version useful on its own when published.
- [ ] Direct useful answer, actionable details and honest limits.
- [ ] Facts verified from current official sources; contradictions resolved or omitted.
- [ ] Visible Amr Elbusaily authorship with background link; no invented experience.
- [ ] Contextual related-article/service/consultation links; destinations verified live.
- [ ] Title, description, canonical, reciprocal language alternatives and sitemap entry checked.
- [ ] Structured data matches visible content. FAQ rich results are no longer shown by Google; FAQs may still help readers. Do not promise CTR or AI citation gains from FAQ markup.
- [ ] Build and relevant code checks; distinguish pre-existing errors from new ones.
- [ ] Production text/source readback and actual visual inspection at useful desktop/mobile widths.
- [ ] Only report resolved live URLs. Publication is not proof of Google indexing, ranking or sales.
